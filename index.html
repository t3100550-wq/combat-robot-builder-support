Purchase

Full Game Unlock

Combat Robot Builder uses one permanent, non-consumable in-app purchase to unlock the full game. There are no subscriptions, no ads and no paid Scrap or Gears.

Restore

Changed device?

Open the Full Game purchase screen and use Restore Purchase. Purchases are handled by Apple and remain tied to the Apple Account used to buy them.

Multiplayer

Game Center 1v1

Online 1v1 uses Apple Game Center. Make sure you are signed into Game Center and have a working internet connection. Match availability depends on another player being available.

Progress

Scrap & Gears

Scrap and Gears are earned through gameplay. They are not sold for real money. Use them for progression, parts, blueprints, garage capacity and cosmetic unlocks.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the tutorial?

The interactive tutorial is offered on first launch and can be opened again from the main menu.

Why can’t I access every arena or Online 1v1?

The free version includes a limited set of content. The permanent Full Game Unlock opens the complete game. Online 1v1 also requires completion of the introductory tutorial.

I purchased the Full Game but it is still locked.

Confirm you are signed into the same Apple Account that made the purchase, then use Restore Purchase from the Full Game screen. If it still does not unlock, contact support and include your device model, OS version and a short description of what happened. Do not send passwords or payment card information.

Does buying the Full Game instantly give me every weapon and cosmetic?

No. The purchase unlocks access to the complete game. Progression items, blueprints, parts, cosmetics and rewards are still earned through normal gameplay.

What platforms are supported?

Combat Robot Builder is designed for iPhone, iPad and Mac Catalyst. Platform availability may vary by App Store release.
