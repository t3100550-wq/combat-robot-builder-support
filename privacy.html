Privacy Policy

Privacy, without the mystery.

Effective date: 8 October 2026

Summary: Combat Robot Builder does not directly collect, sell or use personal information for advertising or tracking. The app does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. Game progress is stored locally on your device. Apple services such as Game Center and StoreKit operate under Apple’s own privacy practices.

1. Information we collect

Combat Robot Builder does not directly collect personal data such as your name, email address, precise location, contacts, photos, browsing history, advertising identifiers or payment card information.

The app’s privacy manifest declares no collected data types and no tracking.

2. Local game data

Your game progress, settings, robot configurations, earned Scrap/Gears and related gameplay state are stored locally in the app’s application data on your device. We do not operate a server that receives this local save data.

3. Apple Game Center

Online multiplayer and achievements may use Apple Game Center. When you use Game Center, Apple may process information such as your Game Center identity, multiplayer session information and achievement activity under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your Apple Account password.

4. In-App Purchase

The Full Game Unlock is processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles payment and purchase records. We do not receive or store your payment card details. The app checks Apple-provided purchase entitlement information so it can unlock purchased content and restore eligible purchases.

5. Nearby / local multiplayer

Local multiplayer features may use Apple’s Multipeer Connectivity framework to discover and communicate with nearby devices. Peer/session information is exchanged directly between participating devices for gameplay and is not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

6. Advertising, analytics and tracking

Combat Robot Builder does not display third-party advertisements and does not use cross-app tracking. The app is not designed to sell personal information or share personal information with data brokers or advertisers.

7. Children’s privacy

Combat Robot Builder does not knowingly request personal information from children. Any Game Center or purchase activity is handled through Apple services and the Apple Account settings applicable to the device.

8. Data retention and deletion

Because gameplay data is stored locally, removing the app removes its local app data subject to the operating system’s normal behaviour and any device backups you control. Game Center records and App Store purchase history are managed by Apple and are not controlled by us.

9. Third-party services

The app relies on Apple platform services including Game Center and StoreKit. Apple’s handling of information through those services is governed by Apple’s privacy policies and terms.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change materially, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or alongside the relevant app update.
